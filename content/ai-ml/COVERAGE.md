# AI & ML coverage audit

**Audit started:** 2026-09-28. A model topic is not complete until data, evaluation, limitations, deployment and responsible-use implications are taught alongside the algorithm.

- [~] Linear algebra, probability, statistics and Python data tools — **shallow**
- [ ] Data collection, quality, leakage, splitting, feature engineering and reproducibility — **missing**
- [ ] Supervised learning: regression, classification, metrics, calibration and uncertainty — **missing**
- [ ] Unsupervised learning, dimensionality reduction, anomaly detection and clustering validation — **missing**
- [ ] Optimization, regularization, bias/variance, cross-validation and hyperparameter search — **missing**
- [ ] Neural networks, CNNs, sequence models, transformers and training stability — **missing**
- [ ] NLP, embeddings, retrieval, LLM prompting, fine-tuning concepts and evaluation — **missing**
- [ ] Responsible AI: fairness, privacy, safety, interpretability, security and governance — **missing**
- [ ] MLOps: experiments, versioning, registry, serving, monitoring, drift and rollback — **missing**
- [ ] End-to-end capstone: a reproducible, evaluated and monitored ML product — **missing**

**Current conclusion:** AI & ML is a structured outline, not a complete mastery curriculum yet.
